# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React/Vite frontend; Python 3.11 FastAPI backend.

## Users

Primary users are founders, small-business owners, and lean marketing leads
who need to launch a product campaign without coordinating separate research,
strategy, creative, advertising, and analytics tools.

## Product Purpose

Kloppy is an AI Marketing Officer that turns a business and product brief into
audience insights, a campaign strategy, coordinated creative assets,
authorized campaign actions, and performance-informed next steps.

## Positioning

Kloppy's differentiator is the coordination layer: it preserves one campaign
context from audience evidence through strategy, creative, execution, and
learning instead of producing isolated marketing outputs.

## Operating Context

Users work through a web workspace to create a campaign brief, review Qloo
cultural-affinity signals and audience hypotheses, approve a strategy, review
creative variations, authorize an execution action, and inspect performance
feedback.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The MVP focuses on one end-to-end campaign workflow.
- Qloo signals inform hypotheses and must not be represented as purchase
  intent or verified individual attributes.
- Creative output may include ad graphics, social content, presentations,
  landing-page copy, email content, and ad copy.
- Publishing, spending, material targeting changes, and permissions require
  explicit authorization.
- Live integrations may be unavailable; simulated actions and metrics must be
  clearly labelled.
- Product facts, audience evidence, strategy versions, assets, approvals,
  execution status, and metrics should remain linked.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Kloppy. The voice should be clear, evidence-aware,
practical, and transparent about uncertainty and system state.

## Product Principles

- Evidence before confidence.
- One campaign context from insight to action.
- Approval before consequential action.
- Honest distinction between live, pending, failed, and simulated states.
- Orchestrate specialist capabilities instead of rebuilding every creative
  editor.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web workspace must provide readable contrast, keyboard-accessible core
controls, meaningful status labels, visible focus states, and must not infer or
target sensitive personal traits.
